Exercise 3.4

$ grep -c "203.0.113.1" case/logs/auth.log
18

$ ./check_ip.sh case/logs/auth.log 203.0.113.1
NOT FOUND: 203.0.113.1 does not appear in case/logs/auth.log
exit: 3

Explanation:
grep -c "203.0.113.1" treats the text as a regular expression and matches it
anywhere in a line. The dots match any character, and 203.0.113.1 is a substring
of longer addresses such as 203.0.113.10 and 203.0.113.100. So it counts lines
for other IPs as well.
check_ip.sh uses grep -wF. -F treats the dots as literal dots, and -w requires
a whole-word match, so only the exact address 203.0.113.1 counts. That address
is not actually in the log, so the script reports NOT FOUND.
